FAQs

How do you choose external brands or artists for co-branded collections?

We seek creative partners who share a mutual respect for craftsmanship, high-street culture, or sub-cultural aesthetics. The ideal partnership must tell a compelling visual story, blending our signature edge with the collaborator’s distinct identity rather than just swapping logos.

How can I collaborate with the brand or become an ambassador?

 We love connecting with creators who match our energy. Whether you are a stylist, influencer, or visual artist, feel free to reach out to our team at fashion@mamc.com or DM us on Instagram with your portfolio.

 What makes your quality different from generic fast fashion?

We do not believe in disposable clothing. We focus on heavy-weight cottons, ultra-soft custom knits, and meticulously reinforced stitching. Every drop is produced in limited runs to minimize waste, prioritize craftsmanship, and keep your wardrobe exclusively unique.

Where are your products designed and manufactured?

All our collections are conceptually conceptualized by our independent in-house design team. We partner closely with premium, vetted manufacturers who specialize in advanced garment techniques—such as intricate jacquard knitting, distressed raw hems, and high-density graphic printing—to ensure every piece feels as premium as it looks.

 What is the story and inspiration behind the brand?

Born out of high-street culture and Y2K aesthetic nostalgia, our brand blends subversive design with daily wearability. We bridge the gap between gritty cyberpunk/wasteland elements and hyper-feminine, romantic details, creating wearable art for those who refuse to blend in.
