FAQs
Everything you need to know about shopping, shipping, and sizing.

How do I find my perfect size across different styles?

Please refer to the specific "Size Guide" on each product page. Our fits range from tight baby tees to oversized drop-shoulder silhouettes, so we highly recommend measuring your favorite garment at home and comparing it to our size chart before ordering.

 When will my order be processed and shipped?

Standard orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Once shipped, a tracking number will be emailed to you instantly. For pre-order or limited-edition items, please check the specific shipping timeline stated on the product description page.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a 14-day hassle-free return or exchange window from the date of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned in their original packaging with all designer tags attached.

Can I modify or cancel my order after it has been placed?

To ensure fast delivery, we process orders quickly. You can modify or cancel your order within 1 hour of purchase by contacting our support team. Once the order enters the shipping stage, it cannot be changed.
